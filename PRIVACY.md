# AuthorizedMC Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

This policy covers the **AuthorizedMC Launcher**, the **AuthorizedMC Client** (a Fabric mod for Minecraft: Java Edition) and the **AuthorizedMC cosmetics service**, all made for the AuthorizedMC Minecraft network (authorizedmc.net). AuthorizedMC is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

## Summary
- We don't sell your data, show ads, or run analytics or tracking.
- Your Microsoft sign-in stays on your computer, encrypted.
- The only thing our servers store about you is your Minecraft profile ID, username and the cosmetics you choose.

## What the Launcher handles
**Microsoft account sign-in.** When you sign in, the launcher uses Microsoft's official sign-in. It receives sign-in tokens from Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft Services so it can check that you own Minecraft: Java Edition and launch the game. These tokens are stored **only on your computer**, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. They are never sent to AuthorizedMC.

**Game downloads.** The launcher downloads Minecraft and Java from Mojang's official servers, Fabric from fabricmc.net, mods from Modrinth, and the AuthorizedMC Client from our GitHub releases. Like any download, these services see your IP address under their own privacy policies.

**Settings.** Your launcher settings (memory, window size and similar) are stored on your computer only.

## What the Client and cosmetics service handle
If cosmetics are turned on, the client connects to our cosmetics service (cosmetics.authorizedmc.net) to share cosmetics between AuthorizedMC Client players:
- **Sign-in check:** to prove you own your account, the client uses Mojang's standard session check (the same one Minecraft servers use). Your Microsoft tokens are sent only to Mojang, never to us.
- **What we store:** your Minecraft profile ID (UUID), your Minecraft username, the cosmetics you've equipped, your custom cape design (colors only), whether capes are shown, and when you were last online with the client.
- **Why:** so other AuthorizedMC Client players can see your cosmetics and the AuthorizedMC crown next to your name in the tab list.
- **Lookups:** your client sends the profile IDs of players near you or in your tab list to the cosmetics service, to fetch their cosmetics. These lookups are not stored.

**Tier Tagger (optional).** If you turn on Tier Tagger, your client sends the profile IDs of players in your tab list to MCTiers (mctiers.com) and PvPTiers (pvptiers.com) to show their PvP tiers.

**Voice chat (optional).** Voice chat is provided by the Simple Voice Chat mod and the server you play on. AuthorizedMC does not receive or store voice audio.

**Local data.** Your client settings, HUD layout and cosmetics choices are stored on your computer in `config/authorizedmc.json`.

## Third parties
Microsoft, Xbox Live, Mojang, Cloudflare (which protects and routes our cosmetics service), Modrinth, GitHub, MCTiers and PvPTiers each process data under their own privacy policies.

## Your choices and deleting your data
- **Turn cosmetics off** in the launcher or in the client (Client Settings > Cosmetics & Locker). The client then stops contacting the cosmetics service.
- **Uninstalling the launcher** removes its saved sign-in, and you can choose to delete all game files too.
- **Remove your account** in the launcher's Accounts page to delete its saved sign-in.
- **Delete your cosmetics data from our server:** contact us (below) with your Minecraft username and we'll delete it. Data for players who stop using the client is deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

## Children
AuthorizedMC is meant for players old enough to use Minecraft under Microsoft's account rules. We don't knowingly collect more than the profile ID, username and cosmetics described above from anyone.

## Changes
